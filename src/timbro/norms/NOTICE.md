# concreteness_brysbaert2014.csv.gz

Source: Brysbaert, M., Warriner, A. B., & Kuperman, V. (2014). Concreteness ratings for 40 thousand generally known English word lemmas. _Behavior Research Methods_, 46, 904-911. https://doi.org/10.3758/s13428-013-0403-5

Originally distributed by the Ghent University Center for Reading Research at crr.ugent.be/archives/1330 (page unreachable as of 2026-07; mirrored from https://github.com/ArtsEngine/concreteness, a verbatim redistribution of the same file, `Concreteness_ratings_Brysbaert_et_al_BRM.txt`).

License: CC BY 4.0 (as stated by the original distribution).

This file keeps only the single-word lemma rows (`Bigram == 0`) and two columns from the original 9: `lemma` (`Word`) and `concreteness` (`Conc.M`, the mean concreteness rating, 1-5 scale). 37,058 rows, gzip-compressed CSV. Dropped columns (SD, unknown count, SUBTLEX frequency, dominant POS, and the 2,896 two-word-expression rows) are not needed for per-lemma lookup.
