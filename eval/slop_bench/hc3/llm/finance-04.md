It can be beneficial to convert non-investment real estate to a rental property if you need to make major repairs, as long as the cost of the repairs is not too high in comparison to the expected rental income. Renting out the property can provide a steady stream of income to offset the cost of the repairs, and it can also potentially increase the value of the property over time.Before making the decision to convert the property to a rental, you should carefully consider the costs and potential risks involved. Some of the factors to consider include the cost of the repairs, the expected rental income, the potential for vacancies and other expenses, and the local market conditions for rental properties. You should also be aware of any legal requirements for owning and operating a rental property in your area, including any necessary licenses or permits.It may also be a good idea to consult with a real estate professional or financial advisor to get more detailed information and advice on the feasibility of converting your property to a rental.
