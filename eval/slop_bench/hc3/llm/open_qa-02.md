A surveyor's wheel, also known as a surveyor's measuring wheel or a trundle wheel, is a handheld instrument that is used to measure distance. It consists of a wheel with a handle and a counter that measures the number of revolutions of the wheel. Surveyors use surveyor's wheels to measure the distance between two points, typically when conducting land surveys or construction surveys. They are a quick and easy way to measure long distances without the need for specialized equipment or a tape measure. Surveyor's wheels are also useful for estimating distances in other applications, such as determining the distance between trees in a forest or the length of a hiking trail.
